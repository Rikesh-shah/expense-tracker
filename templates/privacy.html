Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep your financial data safe.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Your expenses are personal, and we treat them that way. This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking service. It should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions.

1Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the service:

  • Account details — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card details or other payment credentials.

2How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries, breakdowns and filtered views.
  • Keep the service secure and fix problems when they occur.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We never sell your data, and we do not use your expense information for advertising.

3Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and is accessible only to your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never kept in plain text. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expenses and personal details are permanently removed.

4Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties for their own purposes. We may rely on trusted providers — for example, for hosting or loading web fonts — that process limited technical data on our behalf. These providers are only permitted to use it to deliver their service to us.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

5Your Rights

You can view, edit or delete your expense data at any time from within the app. You may also request a copy of your data or the deletion of your account by contacting us.

6Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. We will update the "Last updated" date above whenever this policy changes.
